## About

I’m a computational neuroscientist connecting brain-network research with reproducible computing, scientific data standards, and collaborative research programs.

My work spans cognitive neuroscience, neuroimaging, and open science—from understanding how brain networks support cognition to developing tools and practices that make research more accessible and reproducible.

## Research & Open Science

My interests include:

- Functional and structural brain connectivity
- Cognitive control and episodic memory
- Neuroimaging analysis and quantitative meta-analysis
- Reproducible workflows and research computing
- Scientific data standards, harmonization, and governance

I contribute to collaborative initiatives including **brainlife.io**, the **NIH BRAIN CONNECTS APEX Data Coordinating Center**, **BRIDGE**, and the **Brain Imaging Data Structure (BIDS)**.
